## What gets included

The original post has 5,000+ views, the images, GIF or video are attached to it, and the creator says it was made with ChatGPT Images 2.6. Reposts, official demos and platform promotions are left out. Model attribution is as stated by the creator, not independently reproduced. Prompts come only from the creator (the post, their own reply, a screenshot in a reply, or a link they shared) and are kept verbatim.
